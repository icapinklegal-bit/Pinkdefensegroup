Houston drug charge defense

Possession through trafficking, state and federal. Most drug cases turn on the search, and that's where we start.

Drug charges we defend

We defend cases involving marijuana, cocaine, heroin, methamphetamine, fentanyl, MDMA, LSD, prescription opioids and Xanax. How serious the charge is depends on the substance, its penalty group, the weight and the circumstances of the arrest.

  • Possession. The State must prove you knew about the drugs and controlled them. Drugs found in a shared car or home are often contested.
  • Possession with intent to deliver. Prosecutors try to infer intent from quantity, packaging, scales or cash. Personal use is a common defense.
  • Delivery. Selling, transferring, transporting or offering to sell.
  • Manufacturing. Often built on chemicals, equipment, residue testing and surveillance.
  • Paraphernalia. Frequently added onto other charges.
  • Trafficking. Large-scale cases may be prosecuted in state or federal court.

Possible penalties

LevelRange
Misdemeanor (e.g., small amounts of marijuana)Fine and up to 1 year in county jail
State jail felony (e.g., under 1 gram, Penalty Group 1)180 days to 2 years in state jail
Second- and third-degree felonies2 to 20 years in prison
First-degree and enhanced felonies5 to 99 years or life

Prior convictions, drug-free zones and weapons can raise the level. Ask us how the specific weight and penalty group affect your case.

How drug cases are defended

  • Illegal search and seizure. Warrantless searches, bad warrants and unlawful traffic stops can lead to evidence being thrown out.
  • Knowledge and control. Being near drugs is not the same as possessing them.
  • Lab testing. We review testing methods, contamination and chain of custody.
  • Statements. Statements taken in violation of your right to silence or counsel can be excluded.

Alternatives to conviction

Depending on your history and the charge, options may include drug court, treatment programs, pretrial diversion, deferred adjudication or community supervision. Many of these can protect your record.
