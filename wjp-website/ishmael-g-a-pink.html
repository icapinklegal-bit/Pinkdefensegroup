Ishmael G.A. Pink

Partner. Two-time University of Texas graduate, third generation of the Pink family in law.

The next generation

Ishmael G.A. Pink is a partner at Walter J. Pink & Associates, P.C. and the grandson of its founder, Walter J. Pink. He earned both his undergraduate degree and his Doctor of Jurisprudence from the University of Texas.

He started at the firm as a law clerk and worked his way to partner, learning the practice from inside a family that has defended Houstonians for more than 50 years. He pairs that tradition with a modern, forward-looking approach to case strategy.

Practice focus

  • Defense of people facing serious criminal charges, with a particular focus on DWI and DUI
  • Complex negotiations with prosecutors and opposing parties
  • Personal injury claims against large insurance companies
  • Business formation and planning

How he works

Ishmael's clients describe counsel that is both strategic and compassionate. He keeps clients informed, explains each step in plain terms, and prepares every case as if it will be tried.
