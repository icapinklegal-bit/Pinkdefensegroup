Unlawful Disclosure of Intimate Material

Request Your Free Consultation

  2. Sex Crimes
  3. Unlawful Disclosure of Intimate Material

Texas prohibits disclosing intimate visual material of another person without consent when the material was obtained or created under circumstances in which the person expected it to remain private, and when disclosure causes harm and reveals the person's identity. It is a state jail felony. Threatening to disclose such material can also be a crime.

These cases often grow out of breakups and involve questions about consent, who posted the material, and account security.

Possible Penalties Under Texas Law

SituationOffense levelConfinement rangeMaximum fine
Unlawful disclosureState jail felony180 days to 2 years in state jail$10,000

General ranges under Texas Penal Code §§ 12.21–12.35. Enhancements for prior convictions, deadly weapons, or protected locations can raise these ranges. Probation, deferred adjudication, or diversion may be available depending on the facts.

Defending Unlawful Disclosure of Intimate Material Cases in Houston & Fort Bend County

Sex-offense allegations carry life-changing consequences, including possible sex-offender registration. We act discreetly and early, before any interview with police, and we examine forensic interviews, SANE exams, digital evidence, delayed-outcry timelines, and motives to fabricate. We retain qualified experts when the science matters.

In Houston, most felony and misdemeanor cases are heard at the Harris County Criminal Justice Center at 1201 Franklin Street downtown, and arrests are processed through the Harris County Joint Processing Center. In Fort Bend County, cases are handled at the Fort Bend County Justice Center in Richmond. Arresting agencies include the Houston Police Department, Harris County Sheriff's Office and constables, the Fort Bend County Sheriff's Office, Sugar Land, Missouri City, Rosenberg, Stafford and Katy police, and the Texas Department of Public Safety. Each court and prosecutor's office has its own practices and diversion programs, and knowing them is part of building a strategy.
